Talkers

Every return from the laptop carries a quiet marker tone, so you can hear (and the analysis can see) when a listener is on the laptop path. A tripped talker stays on dry until it is re-armed.

Inject a fault

Stop heartbeat: an app-level fault; the Riedel should drop to dry after the VOX hold time. Silence all outputs: what the Riedel sees if the laptop, soundcard or network dies; PROBE is lost too, so the app disarms. Mute talker returns: heartbeat stays on but voices vanish; shows what the per-talker watchdog must catch. Freeze: misses audio deadlines; shows what the soundcard does when the app hangs. Crash: the process exits; restart it and check it comes back disarmed.
